# 📊 Amazon India Sales & Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an Excel-based management dashboard designed to present to a non-technical stakeholder. The goal is to provide a clear, concise, and high-level overview of Amazon India's business performance, highlighting sales trends, product categories, revenue loss, and regional metrics.

## 🔍 Key Performance Areas Covered
1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Units Sold, Average Order Value, Profit Margin %, and Monthly Trends.
2. Category & Product Performance: Category-wise breakdown of sales, profit, units sold, and the Top 5 Products.
3. Order Status & Revenue Loss: Tracking delivered, shipped, returned, and cancelled orders alongside return and cancellation rates.
4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance:** Evaluation of payment methods, fulfillment channels, and state-wise performance mapping.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Data Modeling, and KPI cards.
